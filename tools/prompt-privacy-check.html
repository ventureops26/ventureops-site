Check before you paste into AI.

This quick check flags common personal and sensitive details that may be worth removing from a prompt. Your text stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

The checker is a simple aid, not a guarantee. Always use your organisation's data and AI policies.

Safer prompting in three steps

  1. Replace real names with roles, initials or fictional placeholders.
  2. Remove contact details, account numbers, exact dates of birth and identifiers.
  3. Keep confidential workplace, health, safeguarding and financial information out unless your approved system and policy explicitly allow it.

Try: “Rewrite this message to a customer about a delayed appointment” instead of including the customer's name, address and booking reference.
