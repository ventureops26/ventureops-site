# Venture Ops — Decision Log

## 4 October 2026 — Keep EXP-001 free while testing willingness to pay

**Context:** The checker has been public for roughly one day. There are no feedback emails, enquiries or payments, which is not enough evidence to justify a paid toolkit.

**Decision:** Keep the checker free. Prepare a transparent optional-support offer instead of creating an unvalidated paid bundle.

**Why:** This provides a direct willingness-to-pay signal without creating a delivery promise or ongoing owner workload.

**Constraint:** A live Stripe product and payment link require explicit owner approval before activation.

**Financial impact:** £0 spend; £100 experiment reserve untouched.


## 5 October 2026 — Activate EXP-001 optional-support checkout

**Context:** The owner explicitly approved the previously prepared live Stripe product and payment route.

**Decision:** Create a customer-set one-time support product (£1–£100, preset £3), publish its Stripe-hosted Payment Link on the free checker, and promise no additional product or service.

**Why:** This creates a direct willingness-to-pay signal with no advertising spend, subscription commitment or fulfilment workload.

**Financial impact:** £0 spend; £100 experiment reserve untouched.
